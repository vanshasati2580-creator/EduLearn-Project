About EduLearn

EduLearn is a modern e‑learning platform built with Flask that helps learners discover, enroll, and progress through curated courses. It features role-based access (student, instructor, admin), engaging course pages with lessons and quizzes, discussion threads, wishlists, and a demo checkout flow.

We focus on a clean, accessible UI with subtle, purposeful motion. Animations respect user preferences and keyboard interactions. The platform is continuously improved with thoughtful micro‑interactions and performance-conscious design.

Our Mission

Make high‑quality education accessible and enjoyable through clear design, practical features, and inclusive experiences.
